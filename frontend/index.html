Blood Cancer Identification using Hybrid Ensemble Deep Learning Technique

J. Jayachitra and N. Umarkathaf, International Conference on Electronics and Renewable Systems (ICEARS), IEEE, 2023. IEEE Xplore 10084996

Three fine-tuned CNNs (MobileNetV2, EfficientNetB0, DenseNet121) are combined by weighted soft voting and a stacked logistic-regression meta-classifier to classify peripheral blood smear images into four Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia classes.

    The paper
    
          
    J. Jayachitra and N. Umarkathaf, “Blood Cancer Identification using Hybrid Ensemble Deep Learning Technique,” ICEARS 2023, IEEE (Xplore 10084996). The paper fine-tunes several pretrained CNNs and combines them into a hybrid ensemble to identify Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia from peripheral blood smear images. This project replicates the approach with three backbones and two ensemble heads, and reports every number from a script run.

    Dataset
    
            
    M. Aria et al., “Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia (ALL) image dataset,” Kaggle, 2021. Blood Cells Cancer (ALL). 3,242 images: Benign, [Malignant] early Pre-B, [Malignant] Pre-B, [Malignant] Pro-B.
    
            
    Method references: C. Mondal et al., weighted CNN ensemble for ALL, arXiv:2105.03995 · R. R. Selvaraju et al., Grad-CAM, ICCV 2017.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    Limitations
    
            
      
              
    • One public dataset of pre-cropped single cells, so there is no segmentation stage and no external validation.
      • 
              
    • The classes are imbalanced. Class weights help, but read per-class recall next to accuracy.
      • 
              
    • Smoke and demo runs produce real script output on toy data. Their numbers mean nothing.
      • 
              
    • Not clinically validated. Do not use for diagnosis.
